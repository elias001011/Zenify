<i>Zenify</i> is the community edition of Musify: a free music player that streams and downloads from YouTube, with no ads and no account.

* Search by name or YouTube link, with suggestions
* Download songs and listen offline, with a download manager
* Playlists with multi-selection, drag to reorder, and Spotify CSV import
* Synced lyrics, queue, sound waves and an equalizer
* Moods, personal Mix and artist or playlist radios
* Local Sync between your Android, Linux and Windows devices, with no cloud
* Listening recap, backup and restore
* Dynamic colours and a background that follows your albums
* Available in 21 languages
